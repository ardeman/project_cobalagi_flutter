Coba Lagi helps children learn the basics of coding through play. Kids put picture blocks in order to guide their little friend along a path to the flag. No reading needed: every instruction is spoken aloud, in Bahasa Indonesia and English.

IT STARTS WITH A WARM-UP GAME
A short, picture-based game finds the right starting point for your child: reading, counting, left and right, patterns and order. It feels like play, not a test. Afterwards, these games stay on the Warm-up island, together with colours and shapes, for little ones to play any time.

NINE CODING ISLANDS
• Directions: forward, turn left, turn right
• Step by step: putting steps in the right order
• Loops: using the repeat block to do more with fewer blocks
• Magic Block: building your own block, then using it again and again
• Look Ahead: checking whether the path is clear before taking a step
• Step Box: saving a number of steps and using it again
• Fix it!: finding and fixing the block that isn't quite right
• Until the flag: a repeat that keeps going until the flag
• Otherwise: if the path is clear, take a step; otherwise, turn
Every island starts with a short demo that shows the idea. Stars on the map show how far your child has come on each island. Completed lessons can be replayed from their island, and every finished island and Warm-up game adds a sticker to the child's sticker book.

PICTURE BLOCKS AND CODE
Children who are still learning to read use picture blocks. Readers can switch to the Code tab and type the same program as text. Blocks can be dragged or tapped to add them, on tablets and phones.

LEARNING AT YOUR CHILD'S PACE
After every puzzle, Coba Lagi picks what comes next: the next island, a fresh practice puzzle, or a bonus adventure to review an earlier skill. Practice puzzles are generated and always checked to be solvable, so a child never gets the same puzzle twice.

MISTAKES ARE PART OF LEARNING
The name means "try again". Every attempt is met with encouragement, never with "wrong". When your child is stuck, the light bulb shows the way.

FOR PARENTS
• The parent area sits behind a question meant for grown-ups
• Choose a child's starting island or replay the warm-up game
• Set a break reminder: after 15, 30 or 45 minutes of puzzles, your child is invited to rest, and only a grown-up can continue
• Sponsors can see a progress report for each child
• Works without an internet connection

FREE, WITH NO ADS
Coba Lagi is free for one player. A donation of any size unlocks sponsor features, such as up to six players on one device. Donations can only be made from the parent area and are handled by Google Play.

PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking, no accounts. Nicknames and progress stay on your device, and the app has no internet access.
